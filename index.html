01 / THE PROBLEM

The cost problem acquirers face

Merchants are priced on a blend. Schemes charge on a BIN. Every transaction where those two disagree is margin you either earn or hand back — and today almost nobody sees the difference in time to act on it.

01

Blended rates hide the cost structure

A single merchant rate averages away the expensive BINs. Portfolio margin looks healthy while individual segments quietly run at or below cost.

02

IC++ varies wildly by BIN

Two cards presented at the same terminal can differ 2–3x in true cost depending on issuer, product tier and region. The difference never reaches the pricing model.

03

Manual routing doesn't scale

Spreadsheet-maintained routing tables go stale the moment a scheme publishes new rates. Errors surface a quarter later, in reconciliation.

04

Reporting arrives too late to matter

Monthly cost reports explain what already happened. By the time the variance is visible, the volume it applied to has settled.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, one decision loop

The platform sits beside your switch. It reads what a transaction actually costs, picks the channel, and feeds the outcome back into the next decision.

STEP 1

Ingest BIN data

Scheme tables, issuer files and your own settlement history land in one normalized BIN record, refreshed as rates publish.

STEP 2

Analyze cost tiers

Each BIN is scored against every channel available to it, including interchange, scheme fees and downgrade risk.

STEP 3

Route intelligently

A routing decision returns in under 40ms with the least-cost viable channel, respecting approval-rate and compliance guardrails.

STEP 4

Report & optimize

Realized versus modelled cost is reconciled per BIN daily, and the variance retrains the next routing decision.
